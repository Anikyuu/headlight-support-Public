Your words and photos stay with their date and time, so you can return to the day as it happened. 「好き」を持つと、
　　心は軽くなる。 Hold on to what you love,
and your heart feels lighter.

好きな音楽。心に残った映画。何度も読み返したい言葉。
そこには、自分が何に心を動かされ、何を大切にしているかが表れています。 Music you love. A film that stayed with you. Words you want to return to again and again.
They reveal what moves you and what matters to you.

好きなものをそばに置くことは、自分の心が向かう先を思い出す、小さなきっかけになります。 Keeping what you love close can be a small reminder of where your heart wants to go.

Head-Lightは、好きなものや言葉をそばに残すことで、自分の「好き」を見失わず、少し軽い心で日々を進めていくための手帳です。 Head-Light is a journal that keeps what you love and the words that matter close, helping you move through each day with a lighter heart without losing sight of yourself.
