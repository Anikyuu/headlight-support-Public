# Heavy to light

Selected by Kai: the colored, exaggerated comic showing thoughts as a heavy burden, speaking into a phone, then floating with relief. Created with the built-in image generation tool; the later line-only variation was not selected. The source PNG is copied unchanged into this folder.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: a memorable, humorous three-panel comic for the Head-Light app's white landing page.
Primary idea: VISIBLY turn a mentally HEAVY person into a physically LIGHT person by speaking thoughts into a phone. Deliberate comic exaggeration. This must make someone smile; avoid an ordinary business illustration.
Format: one very wide 3:1 strip, exactly three equal square scenes, readable left to right. Pure white background. Keep each scene fully inside its own third, with white gutters and no panel borders. Show full bodies and feet; do not crop the action.
Same character in all three scenes: a relatable adult with short tousled dark hair, pale sky-blue everyday shirt, simple charcoal trousers and ordinary shoes. Expressive humorous face and adult caricature proportions, around four heads tall. Warm, lively hand-drawn manga/editorial cartoon linework, confident slightly irregular charcoal ink, restrained light blue and gray flat washes. Not a handsome anime portrait, not corporate clip art, not photoreal, not 3D.
LEFT / HEAVY: An absurdly ENORMOUS dense charcoal-gray bundle of thoughts, at least twice the person's torso size, is weighing down the top of their head and shoulders like a boulder. The bundle visibly consists of tangled scribbles, crumpled papers, blank calendar sheets and several tiny unlabelled reminder cards. The person is squashed into a comical deep hunch with compressed knees, shoulders down, a strained but funny expression and one sweat drop. One hand holds a phone low; the other is trying to support the huge burden. Small ground compression marks make the weight obvious. No pain or injury.
CENTER / LET IT OUT: The same person now holds the phone near their mouth and SPEAKS. A wonderfully absurd ribbon of tangled thoughts and little blank note sheets pours out with their speech and funnels into the phone, whose visible white screen has one large simple microphone symbol. The giant thought burden above them visibly unravels and gets much smaller. Their body is springing back up, eyes bright with surprise. Make the human hand, open speaking mouth, flowing paper ribbon, and phone gesture especially clear. The paper stream must go INTO the phone.
RIGHT / LIGHT: The burden is completely gone. The same person is now so light that both feet float a little above the ground, with a small separated shadow underneath. Relaxed shoulders, buoyant happy posture, a broad relieved smile, a little upward breeze in shirt and hair. They still hold the phone safely and casually. On its tiny screen, only a few neat gray note strokes and a small check symbol. Their posture says 'Ahhh, that's lighter!' without any words. Keep the buoyancy comical and charming, not superhero flight.
Mood: playful visual metaphor for releasing the effort of remembering, with a clear before / speaking / relieved story. The comic itself carries the idea without explanation.
ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numerals, captions, speech balloons, logos or watermarks. Text will be placed separately in HTML in eight languages.
Avoid: an ordinary person merely looking at a phone three times; static polite smiles; subtle changes; busy backgrounds; stock business scenes; money symbolism; dark page backgrounds; gold; gradients or decorative blobs; extra people. Prioritize strong silhouette, exaggerated weight and visible floating relief.
